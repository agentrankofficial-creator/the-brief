EDITOR'S READ
Suhail Seth · May 12, 2026

The story this evening is not just that Trump put the Iran ceasefire on "1% chance of living" — it is that three governments are now visibly running the same playbook on the same shock. Trump moved Monday to suspend the US federal gas tax. Carney suspended Canadian fuel excise April 19. Modi just made his second austerity appeal in 24 hours — asking Indians to skip gold for a year, cut foreign travel, carpool. Three economies, one Strait, one playbook: triage the household budget while the geopolitical chip is still being negotiated.

What I think the consensus is missing: this is not a symmetric shock. The rupee printed a record low 95.63 today, with ₹2.07 lakh crore of FII outflows YTD and Modi's escalating ask-the-citizen austerity rhetoric. Canada — same Hormuz, same fertilizer chain — has potash export leverage, soft CAD that helps exporters, and the Telus/Ottawa sovereign-AI cluster announcement coming through cleanly. The same closure is structurally bullish for CAD-denominated assets and structurally bearish for INR. That asymmetry will widen, not narrow, before it resets. I am sizing my own books accordingly.

Second: OpenAI's DeployCo ($4B raise from TPG, Advent, Bain Capital, Brookfield — with McKinsey, Bain & Co and Capgemini as named partners, plus the Tomoro acquisition) is the most consequential AI move of the week, even on a week with a Trump-Xi summit. The traditional consulting model just invested $4B in its own replacement layer. They decided being inside the disruption beats being outside it. The April 15 "token as labor unit" thesis (P145-3) is no longer a thesis — it is a deployed product with McKinsey's logo on it.

The Beijing summit tomorrow is the safety valve, not the rupture. Trump's CEO delegation — Musk, Cook, Fink, Ortberg, Schwarzman, Solomon, Fraser, Culp, Miebach — signals managed escalation. The communique will be process language. The story will be in the side deals signed at hotel bars. Watch Boeing, Apple, BlackRock.

The investor list (TPG, Advent, Bain Capital, Brookfield + McKinsey, Bain & Co, Capgemini) is not a coincidence — it's the consulting cartel buying optionality on its own obsolescence. Tomoro acquisition adds 150 deployment engineers immediately. Within two quarters, expect at least one Fortune 500 enterprise to disclose "deployed tokens per revenue dollar" as a board KPI. The token-as-labor unit thesis (P145-3, April 15) is now a quarterly OKR.
Time horizon: 6–12 months for first KPI disclosure; 24 months for full vertical-stack restructuring of enterprise consulting
